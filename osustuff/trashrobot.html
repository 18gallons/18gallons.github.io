BULKSORT ROVER

THINK SMARTER

Bulksort Rover features next-generation AI performance. BAND TOGETHER

An integrated WiFi 7 antenna means Bulksort Rover isn't a standalone item. While it is certainly capable on its own, Bulksort Rover can communicate with thousands of other units in your system. With each rover covering up to 15,000 square feet, the mesh can cover massive swathes of its workspace. When multiple Bulksort Rovers are brought together, they form a swarm to communicate with each other and work together. Each Rover's sensor data is shared with every other Rover in your logistics network, meaning the Rovers are as smart as your job is complicated.

WORK HARDER

Bulksort Rover can take on the toughest tasks. Landfills are often full of entirely serviceable items, such as recycleable metals and glass. Landfills are also full of plastic that is not fit for recycling. Bulksort Rover is equipped with a gripper for medium-sized objects, a scoop for soft materials, and a grinder for cutting apart large objects like cars. A robust suspension system with 4 independent tracks allows the Rover to traverse any material in any weather. Bulksort Rover can seamlessly integrate with preexisting logistics networks, but where it shines is within our ecosystem. Our processing facilities can break down plastics using bacterial agents to digest them. This has a massive advantage over traditional methods like incineration, which release millions of tons of CO and CO2 gas into the atmosphere. In comparison, bacterial processing is as harmless as you can get and even generates power in the process.
